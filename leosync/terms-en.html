2. Description of the service

LeoSync is an Android app that backs up and synchronizes photos, videos and other files between an Android device and a PC or NAS on your own network using SMB. Depending on the profile, this works one-way or bidirectionally. Files are transferred directly between the device and the storage location configured by the user. The Developer operates neither a cloud service nor a server for this purpose.
