Android backup without the cloud: back up photos locally to your PC or NAS

With LeoSync you back up Android photos and videos to your own PC or NAS, with no cloud provider, no account and full control over your data.

Benefits of a local backup

  • Your data stays with you: Photos sit on your PC or NAS on your own network, not with a provider.
  • No subscription, no provider storage limit: Capacity is set by your own drive.
  • Fast access at home: Large videos travel over your Wi-Fi instead of your internet connection.
  • Free phone storage: Old files that are verifiably on the PC can be removed from the phone.

Cloud or local: the main differences

Cloud backupLocal backup with LeoSync
Where is the data?With the providerOn your PC or NAS
Storage spaceUsually limited, often paid beyond thatAs large as your own drive
Access on the goUsually easyBy default only on your home network (mobile data/VPN optional)
ResponsibilityWith the providerWith you: the PC or NAS needs a backup of its own

A local backup does not replace a second backup: important photos should also be on another drive in case the PC or NAS fails.

Privacy with LeoSync

  • LeoSync has no server of its own and needs no account.
  • Files only travel between your phone and your PC or NAS. Apart from syncing, the only connection is the update check on GitHub.
  • Passwords are stored encrypted on the phone (Android Keystore).
  • Diagnostic data only leaves the phone if you share it yourself.

Details are in the privacy policy.

Technical requirements

  • Android 10 or newer.
  • A Windows PC or NAS with an SMB share (SMB2/SMB3) and a user with write permission.
  • Phone and PC on the same Wi-Fi.

Setup with LeoSync

  1. Share a folder on the PC (step by step in the help).
  2. Install LeoSync, grant permissions and create a Back up photos & videos profile.
  3. Check with Test connection, then tap Sync once. After that, the backup runs automatically on your home Wi-Fi.

Frequently asked questions

Can I back up Android photos without the cloud?

Yes. LeoSync transfers photos and videos directly via SMB to your own PC or NAS. There is no cloud service and no account.

What data does LeoSync send to the internet?

Apart from syncing with your PC or NAS, only the update check on GitHub. Diagnostic data only leaves the phone if you share it yourself.

Does a local backup replace a second backup?

No. Important photos should also be on another drive, so that a failure of the PC or NAS does not affect everything.

How much does LeoSync cost?

LeoSync is free and open source (MIT licence). The app is currently in beta.
